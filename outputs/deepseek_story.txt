**The Last Letter**  

Emma found the envelope tucked inside an old book—her grandmother’s familiar handwriting on the yellowed paper. With trembling hands, she unfolded the letter.  

*"My dearest Emma, if you’re reading this, I’m gone. But before I left, I had to tell you the truth. The locket you wear—the one you thought was a simple heirloom—holds a secret. Inside the clasp, you’ll find a key."*  

Emma’s breath caught. The silver locket had been her most treasured possession since childhood. She pried it open, revealing a tiny, rusted key hidden in the hinge.  

The letter continued: *"Go to the old oak in the garden. Dig beneath its roots."*  

That night, under a full moon, Emma dug until her fingers struck metal. A small chest emerged, caked in dirt. The key fit perfectly. Inside lay a stack of letters tied with ribbon—each one addressed to her from a father she barely remembered.  

*"He didn’t abandon us,"* her grandmother had written. *"He was taken too soon. These letters were his last gift to you. Forgive me for keeping them. I only wanted to protect you."*  

Tears blurred Emma’s vision as she clutched the letters to her chest. For the first time in years, she didn’t feel alone.  

The past was no longer a mystery—it was a bridge to the love she had never known was waiting for her.
